How this layout works

The main area is col-lg-8 and contains its own row. Nested columns divide the 8-column parent into 12 new columns. The sidebar is col-lg-4. The bottom box uses col-md-8 offset-md-2 col-lg-6 offset-lg-3 to center itself by pushing it right with empty columns.

576px to 991pxNested boxes side by side inside Main; Sidebar below Main. Centered box is 8 of 12 columns from 768px.
